# Thesis #36: SICKLE CELL DISEASE CRISIS MANAGEMENT IN RESOURCE-LIMITED SETTINGS: A SYSTEMATIC REVIEW OF LOW-COST HYDROXYUREA MONITORING PROTOCOLS

**Author:** Kelechi Emeka Ogbonna
**Affiliation:** Independent computational research / Project Confluence
**Format:** B.Sc. project chapter structure (Nile University style)

## Overview
This systematic review thesis addresses the lack of evidence-based guidance for clinicians in resource-limited settings (like Cross River State, Nigeria) regarding the safety of reduced-frequency monitoring for hydroxyurea-treated sickle cell disease patients.

## Background
Building on the foundational work of **Thesis Zero** (BSc *Carica papaya* AgNP, Nile University 2022) and the broader Project Confluence thesis series, this manuscript emphasizes practical, low-cost crisis management strategies in West Africa.
